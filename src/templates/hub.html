Operating systems

How an operating system shares one CPU between many processes. Enter your own processes, press play, and read the reason for every decision.

  • CPU scheduling simulatorAll five algorithms in one place, and how to choose between them
  • First Come First ServedRuns processes in the order they arrive
  • Shortest Job FirstPicks the shortest waiting job when the CPU frees up
  • Shortest Remaining Time FirstInterrupts for any job that would finish sooner
  • Priority schedulingRuns the most important ready process, with or without interrupting
  • Round RobinGives each process a fixed slice in turn
  • Compare scheduling algorithmsAll five on the same processes, side by side
